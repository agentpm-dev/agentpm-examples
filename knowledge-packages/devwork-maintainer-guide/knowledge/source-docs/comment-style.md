# Comment Style

## Explain the decision

Maintainer comments should say what decision is being made, why it is being made, and what will happen next. Even short replies should leave the reporter knowing whether the issue is accepted, needs more detail, or is being redirected.

## Set expectations cleanly

Do not promise a fix date unless one is committed. When closing or deferring an issue, keep the tone respectful and concrete, and point to the next useful action rather than ending with a vague apology.
